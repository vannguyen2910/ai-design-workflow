## Desired outcome
One desired outcome, measurable, within the team's reach (for example: "raise the share of visitors who sign up after pressing Save from X% to Y%"). Name the metric if there is one.

## Tree
Top to bottom: outcome → opportunity (user need, pain point) → solution → assumption to test. Every opportunity must have **evidence** (a test or a source). Every branch should have 2 or more solutions to compare.

```
Outcome: ...
├── Opportunity 1: ...            (evidence: ...)
│   ├── Solution A: ...
│   │   └── Assumption to test: ...   → hypothesis
│   └── Solution B: ...
│       └── Assumption to test: ...   → hypothesis
└── Opportunity 2: ...            (evidence: ...)
    ├── Solution C: ...
    └── Solution D: ...
```

Pick **one** opportunity and 2 to 3 solutions to test first, with the reason. Record the result once there is a decision.

## What this cannot tell us
What this tree does not tell us. The tree reflects what is known when it is drawn and needs updating as new evidence arrives.
