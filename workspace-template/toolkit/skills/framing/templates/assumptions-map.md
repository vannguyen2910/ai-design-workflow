## Scope
The product or idea being considered, and the decision this map helps make (for example: what to test first).

## Assumptions
One sentence per assumption, starting with "We believe that...". Rate **Importance** (how bad if it is wrong) and **Evidence** (how much we have now) on a scale of 1 to 5. Do not write real names or figures that are not anonymised.

| # | Assumption | Group (users / problem / solution / business) | Importance (1-5) | Evidence (1-5) | Source of evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | We believe that... | | | | |

## Priority
An assumption with **high importance and low evidence** needs to be tested first.

| Importance \ Evidence | Little evidence (1-2) | Has evidence (4-5) |
|---|---|---|
| **High importance (4-5)** | Test first: | Keep, monitor: |
| **Low importance (1-2)** | Later: | Ignore: |

## To test
For each high-priority assumption: how to test it, the pass criteria, and the hypothesis (H#) to record.

| Assumption | How to test | Pass criteria | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | | | H? |

## What this cannot tell us
What this map does not tell us. The scores are the team's judgement, not data.
