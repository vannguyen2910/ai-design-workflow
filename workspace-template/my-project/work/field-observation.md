*Discover: understand the problem and people. Method card: fill this in when you run the method.*

## When to use
You suspect people's words differ from their behaviour, or the context (place, tools, interruptions) matters.

## How
1. Define what you are watching and get consent.
2. Observe for 30 to 60 minutes; write what happens, not what you think.
3. Capture workarounds, interruptions and tools used.
4. Afterwards, separate observations from interpretations.
