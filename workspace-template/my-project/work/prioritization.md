## When to use
More than 3 opportunities compete for attention.

## How
1. List the opportunities with their evidence.
2. Rate impact on users and confidence in the evidence (high/medium/low).
3. Add a rough effort estimate with the team.
4. Rank them and write one sentence of reasoning for each.
