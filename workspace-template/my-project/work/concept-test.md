## When to use
You have 2 or 3 directions and need to know which one to take forward.

## How
1. Define what you need to learn and what would change your mind.
2. Prepare the concept and 3 tasks or questions.
3. Run with 5 people; record what they say and do.
4. Summarize per concept: understood, wanted, confused by.
