*Deliver: test, hand off and launch. Method card: fill this in when you run the method.*

## When to use
You are about to launch and need to agree on risk and measurement.

## How
1. Define the audience for each step of the rollout.
2. Link the metrics and targets you will watch.
3. Set the stop or rollback conditions.
4. Agree who decides and who communicates.
