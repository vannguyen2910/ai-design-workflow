## When to use
A live product exists and you want a quantitative baseline before designing.

## How
1. Choose the 3 questions you want the data to answer.
2. Collect the funnel, usage and retention numbers for the same period.
3. Note what the data shows and what it cannot explain.
4. Turn the unexplained parts into questions for interviews or tests.
