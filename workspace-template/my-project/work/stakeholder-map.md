*Discover: understand the problem and people. Method card: fill this in when you run the method.*

## When to use
At the start, or whenever a decision keeps stalling.

## How
1. List everyone who decides, is affected, or can block the work.
2. Mark influence (high/low) and interest (high/low).
3. Write what each one needs and what they are worried about.
4. Decide who to interview first and who to keep informed.
