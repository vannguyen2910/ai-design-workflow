*Discover: understand the problem and people. Method card: fill this in when you run the method.*

## When to use
The team has strong opinions and few facts, or before you invest in research.

## How
1. Write every assumption as one sentence ('We believe that...').
2. Group them: users, problem, solution, business.
3. Place each on two axes: how important if wrong, and how little evidence you have.
4. Pick the top-right ones: important and unproven. These become hypotheses to test.
