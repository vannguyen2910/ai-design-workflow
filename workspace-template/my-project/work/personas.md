*Define: decide which problem is worth solving. Method card: fill this in when you run the method.*

## When to use
You have enough research to see distinct patterns of needs and behaviour.

## How
1. Pick the dimensions that differ most between your users (goal, context, skill).
2. Create 2 to 4 personas; every statement must trace to a source.
3. Write goals, frustrations and the situation they are in.
4. Mark what is evidence and what is assumption.
