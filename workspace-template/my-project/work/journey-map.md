## When to use
You need to see the whole experience, not one screen, and find where to intervene.

## How
1. Choose one persona and one goal.
2. List the steps from trigger to outcome.
3. For each step note actions, thoughts, emotions and touchpoints.
4. Mark the pain points and the moments that matter most.
