How a trip moves through Travel Buddy

Each column is one actor: the traveler, Travel Buddy itself, a local, and a companion or contact. A line that crosses into another column is a handover. Orange marks the key moments and the handover from a local to the traveler.

1. How the parts of the app hand over to each other

Read the numbers on the arrows together with the list below the diagram.

Five stages of the app and the handovers between them.

  1. The three questions at the start set up the feed by travel style and the matching with locals by interest.
  2. One tap saves a tip from the feed to the shortlist.
  3. Every tip shows who wrote it. Tapping a verified local opens their profile and the chat.
  4. Places visited and people met are written into the journal.
  5. After the trip, the next trip starts in the feed again.

2. Before the trip: getting started, Discover and Plan

A visitor can browse without an account; sign-up is only asked for when saving a tip or messaging someone.

3. On the trip: arrival and Connect

The chat with a local opens only when both sides show interest; report or block is available on every profile.

4. After the trip: the journal

User flow from the end of the trip to the next one.

The map shows the main path. Error states and edge cases are not drawn.
