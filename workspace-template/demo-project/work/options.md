*Develop stage.*

> **An honest note:** the original project did **not** formally put the three directions below side by side. They were drawn from the open tensions in the solution pool and the research synthesis, to illustrate the step of choosing a direction.

## Three directions
*At least three directions that are really different. If two directions are only variants of one idea, they count as one.*

| Direction | The idea in one line | Serves | Pulls against | Why not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Planning leads | "One app instead of 8 to 10 apps": the hook is gathering the plan, everything else serves it | Planning | The local-discovery need and the journal are treated as secondary | Competitors such as Google Maps and AI chat tools are moving in here too |
| B. Local discovery leads | The hook is real tips from real people, no ads | Local tips | Hard to keep quality as content grows; easy to commercialise | Needs content volume before it has value |
| C. The journal leads | The hook is memories that organise themselves, privately | Journal | The survey ranks the journal 3rd (3.62/5); the need may be only a wish | The behavioural need has not been validated |

## Chosen direction and why
*Check against the opportunities and the principle checks. Record why the other directions were parked.*

**Discovery is the door, planning is the positioning hook.** The Explore tab is the first entry point (even for visitors who have not signed up) and every tip can be saved into the trip plan with one tap. Planning is prioritised in positioning because it has the highest importance score and is where fragmentation hurts most. The journal and connection are supporting pillars.
