## Scope
One persona, one goal, one situation.

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Persona | A traveller who plans seriously before a trip but hates being locked in |
| Their goal | A trip that feels local, planned without stitching information across many apps, and memories they can find again |
| Situation | A short international trip with family, a partner or close friends |
| Trigger | They see a place on social media or an AI tool and start a planner |
| Desired outcome | Back home with a plan they trusted, a trip they enjoyed, and a record they can revisit |

## Objectives
- Find the stage where the traveller loses the most time and trust today. Our guess: between Consideration and Decision, where they copy information between apps by hand.
- Show which part of Travel Buddy (Discover, Connect, Plan & Journal) serves which stage.
- Give the team one shared picture of the whole trip before choosing what to build first.

## Journey
The five stages follow the trip: **Awareness** (inspiration), **Consideration** (cross-checking), **Decision** (committing to a plan), **Service** (on the trip), **Loyalty** (after the trip). Quotes are taken word for word from the interview. Where we have no answer yet, the cell says so.

## Moments that matter
1. **Between Consideration and Decision: copying by hand.** Every place moves from one app to another manually. The interviewee named this as the one thing they would change, and it is where Travel Buddy can help most.
2. **Consideration: the trust check.** The traveller has their own test: the same place in several sources. A place that appears only once raises suspicion. If Travel Buddy tips are not clearly local and non-commercial, they will go back to cross-checking.
3. **Loyalty: details lost after the trip.** The record is the reason to come back to the app, but only if it costs the traveller no effort.

## Opportunities
- Plan flexibly in one place, instead of juggling many apps (Decision, Service)
- Trustworthy, non-commercial local suggestions (Awareness, Consideration)
- Keep a record of the trip without the effort (Loyalty)
- Connect with locals in a way that is safe, free and non-transactional (Service)
- Get past the barriers to trying the app: price, privacy and a friend network (Consideration to Decision)

## What this cannot tell us
- It rests on one interview and a small survey, mostly young travellers in Asia. The other personas have their own journeys; this map covers one persona only.
- The five stages are applied to a product that does not exist yet. The first two stages describe how travellers plan today, not how they would find Travel Buddy. How they would hear about the app is unknown.
- The KPI targets are the team's own and nobody has confirmed them. Teams involved is empty because we do not yet know who would own each stage.
- Feelings are read from the interview, not observed. No real usability test has been run.
- The experience of locals (the supply side) is not mapped.
