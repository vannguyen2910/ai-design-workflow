## Scope
- The question: how do travellers find, plan and keep a record of a trip, and where does it hurt?

## Themes
*From one person. Only a hypothesis to be confirmed with more participants.*

1. **Fragmentation is the main pain, not a lack of information.** The interviewee uses 8 to 10 tools; the difficulty is copying information between them. *"I wish there was an easier way to gather, organize, and manage everything in one place instead of jumping between so many apps."* 
2. **Cross-checking sources is the personal trust algorithm.** *"If a restaurant shows up on TikTok, Google Maps, travel groups, and is also recommended by someone I know, I feel much more confident about trying it."* 
3. **Wants local knowledge but does not know whom to ask.** *"I don't really know how to find the right local person to ask."* 
4. **A structured plan before the trip, improvised once there.** About 30 to 40% of the trip changes from the plan.
5. **The wish to keep a journal is bigger than the ability to do it.** *"I think, 'I'll remember this later,' or 'I'll organize everything when I get home,' but that doesn't always happen."* 
6. **Sharing stays in a close circle and is functional, not broadcast publicly.** For the interviewee, the trip archive is mainly for remembering.

## Opportunities + limits
Five needs, ordered by the importance users gave to the features (survey):

1. Flexible planning in one place (4.38/5, rank 1)
2. Discovering trustworthy local experiences (3.85/5, rank 2)
3. Keeping memories without the effort (3.62/5, rank 3)
4. Safe, free connection with locals (3.38/5, rank 4)
5. Getting past the barriers to trying it: price, privacy, the friend network

**What this sample cannot tell us:**
- A small sample (13), skewed to Asia and young people; every percentage is directional only.
- One interview: can only be treated as a hint, not a sample.
- The supply side (locals) has not been researched.
- A multiple-choice survey may under-report systemic pains such as fragmentation.
- The need to keep a journal may be a wish and not yet behaviour; diary research is needed.
