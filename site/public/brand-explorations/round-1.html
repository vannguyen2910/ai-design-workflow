Logo marks · round 1

Six mark ideas, independent of direction

Each one hints at a level-up through steps or stages. The accent shape marks the top stage, which is where the course ends: a workflow you reuse. Any mark can be recolored for any direction.

MARK 1

Rising bars

Four bars for four stages, the last one highlighted. Reads as stairs and as progress at once.

MARK 2

Step path

One line climbing in steps to a dot. Simplest and most flexible. Works as a divider on pages too.

MARK 3

Door ziggurat

Stepped pyramid with a doorway at the bottom: the open door and the stairs in one shape.

MARK 4

Arch with steps

A doorway with stairs inside. Calm and architectural. Reads like a gateway to the next level.

MARK 5

Four stages, one gate

Four dots growing along a dotted path, the last one ringed. Ties to the gate at the end of each stage.

MARK 6

Climbing notes

Four slightly tilted sticky squares stepping upward. The most tactile and handmade of the six.

My lean

Direction B with mark 3 or 4. It fits "calm senior mentor" and "sharp practitioner" best, and it's the strongest match for editorial, warm and craft. You can borrow A's bold color blocks or C's sticker details for playful moments, so the page doesn't feel too quiet. Your pick decides, though.
