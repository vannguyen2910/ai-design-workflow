# FoodNest (fictional) — public page

**Delivery fee:** from $1.99, free over $25.

**Delivery:** choose a time slot up to 3 days ahead. You can change the slot until 30 minutes before it starts. If you are not home, choose "leave at the door".

**Cancel and change:** one-tap cancel on the home screen until the restaurant starts cooking. The cut-off time is shown next to the button.

**First order:** a three-question quiz at sign-up (diet, budget, how often). A welcome tip after the first order.

**Delivery estimates:** shows a live estimate with a map and updates it when the courier is delayed.
