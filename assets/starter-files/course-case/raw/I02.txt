Interview I02 (fictional, anonymised). Participant: P2. Each turn has a number; evidence notes cite the number.

01 Interviewer: Tell me about the last time you used the app.
02 P2: Last Thursday, dinner for myself.
03 Interviewer: How did you choose the delivery time?
04 P2: When I picked the delivery time, I assumed I could change it later like in other apps. I only learned it was locked when I tried.
05 Interviewer: And then?
06 P2: I thought cancelling would be easy. It is not obvious. I would rather the app told me the deadline before it charges me.
07 Interviewer: Was that your first order?
08 P2: No, my third. The first time I was not sure the order had gone through. There was no message for a while.
09 Interviewer: How about the price?
10 P2: Honestly, the waiting time bothered me more than the price. After a long day I do not want to wait an hour for dinner.
11 Interviewer: Would you recommend it?
12 P2: To a colleague, yes, with a warning about the time slot.
