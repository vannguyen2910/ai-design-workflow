# Insight set: Delivery App (fictional)

**All insights are invented.** They are what a designer's Lesson 2 work could hand to Lesson 3. Each one has an insight ID, a source type, the evidence note IDs it rests on (see the `evidence/` folder, RET-01 to RET-10), and a strength. Persona, journey map and opportunity lines must cite these insight IDs.

| Insight ID | Insight | Source type | Evidence notes | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IN-01 | New customers do not realise the delivery time slot is final until it is too late, and the order can arrive when they are away | Interview | RET-01, RET-05 | Medium: 2 notes, 2 participants |
| IN-02 | Cancelling or changing an order is hard to find, and the cut-off is not shown where customers cancel | Interview | RET-02, RET-06 | Medium: 2 notes, 2 participants |
| IN-03 | Delivery estimates feel optimistic, and the first order can make a new customer feel something went wrong | Interview | RET-03, RET-07 | Medium: 2 notes, 2 participants |
| IN-04 | Price is not clearly the main reason customers leave: 3 of 10 notes mention it and they point different ways | Interview | RET-08, RET-09, RET-10 | Weak: 3 notes, mixed, and it contradicts IN-07 only in part |
| IN-05 | The number of restaurants to choose from is valued and worth protecting | Interview | RET-04 | Weak: 1 note |
| IN-06 | The business question is why new customers do not reorder after the first order; the 30-day second-order rate is 41% | Scope | none (case card) | Confirmed by business |
| IN-07 | The team believes price is the main reason customers leave | Assumption | none | Unconfirmed: IN-04 is mixed |
| IN-08 | Most new customers are using a delivery app for the first time | Assumption | RET-07 hints at it | Unknown: one note only |
| IN-09 | The time slot and cancel problems hit customers whose plans change or who are away from home | Interview | RET-01, RET-02 | Weak: 2 notes, one participant |

Sample limits: 10 evidence notes, one app, two participants from two interviews, both polite. There are no review or forum notes in this set, so no insight rests on more than one kind of source. Say so in any limits line.

**Changed on 2026-11-20:** this set used to rest on 24 one-line source notes (R, F and I IDs) from three kinds of source. It now rests on the ten evidence notes written from the two interview transcripts. Insight texts are kept close to the old ones; IN-04 and IN-05 changed because the evidence behind them changed.
