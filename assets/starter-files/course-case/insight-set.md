# Insight set: Delivery App (fictional)

**All insights are invented.** They are what a designer's Lesson 2 work could hand to Lesson 3. Each one has an insight ID, a source type, the source IDs it rests on (see `source-notes.md`), and a strength. Persona, journey map and opportunity lines must cite these insight IDs.

| Insight ID | Insight | Source type | Source IDs | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IN-01 | New customers do not realise the delivery time slot is final until it is too late, and food goes cold when they are away | Interview | I01-01, I02-01, R01, F01 | Strong: 4 notes, 3 kinds of source |
| IN-02 | Cancelling or changing an order is hard to find, and the cut-off is not shown where customers cancel | Interview | I01-02, I02-02, R04, F04 | Strong: 4 notes, 3 kinds of source |
| IN-03 | Delivery estimates feel optimistic, and the first order can make a new customer feel something went wrong | Interview | I01-03, I02-03, F05, F06 | Medium: daily annoyance, 4 notes |
| IN-04 | Price is not the main reason customers leave: 2 of 24 notes mention it, one kindly | Interview | R06, F07, I02-03 | Weak: 2 notes, and it contradicts IN-07 |
| IN-05 | Variety and food quality are valued and worth protecting | Interview | R07, R08, F08 | Medium: 3 notes, positive |
| IN-06 | The business question is why new customers do not reorder after the first order; the 30-day second-order rate is 41% | Scope | none (case card) | Confirmed by business |
| IN-07 | The team believes price is the main reason customers leave | Assumption | none | Unconfirmed: IN-04 points the other way |
| IN-08 | Most new customers are using a delivery app for the first time | Assumption | F06 hints at it | Unknown: one note only |
| IN-09 | The time slot and cancel problems hit customers whose plans change or who are away from home | Interview | I01-01, I01-02 | Weak: 2 notes, one interview |

Sample limits: 24 notes, one app, two interviewees. Reviews lean towards strong feelings, the forum is people who had a problem, and the two interviewees are polite. Say so in any limits line.
