# Course project: Delivery App (fictional)

**Everything in this folder is invented.** No real company, person, review or number.

Use it when:
- your company bans AI tools, or your own project is too sensitive for class;
- you missed a week and need to catch up;
- you want a safe project to practise on.

| File | Used in | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `case-card.md` | Week 1 demo and fallback project | One page |
| `po-case-pack.md` | Retired, not used in class | For the student who has no PO to talk to. **The PO-only section is for the person playing the PO** |
| `source-notes.md` | Week 2, session B | 24 fictional source notes with IDs, from three kinds of source |
| `insight-set.md` | Week 3 | Nine fictional insights with insight IDs, source type and strength; the input for persona, journey map and opportunity |
| `persona-ai-draft.md` | Week 3 activity | A six-line persona an AI might write; some lines have an insight behind them, some do not. Answer key inside, instructor only |
| `competitors/` | Week 2 demo | Three fictional competitor pages |
| `brief-notes-plain.txt` | Week 2, Break the brief | 10 dòng brief của business dạng `ID | text` (B01 đến B10), có giả định ngầm và hai chỗ mâu thuẫn |
| `break-the-brief-moves.md` | Week 2, Break the brief | Prompt và kết quả đã lưu của bốn move AI trên brief mẫu, kèm giới hạn |
| `insights-plain.txt`, `persona-draft-plain.txt`, `journey-map-draft.md`, `opportunities-draft-plain.txt` | Week 3, AI moves | Chín insight có ID, sáu dòng persona nháp, một journey map nháp và năm opportunity nháp, dạng `ID | text`, cho bảy AI move |
| `define-moves.md` | Week 3, AI moves | Prompt và kết quả đã lưu của sáu AI move (persona, journey map, opportunity), kèm giới hạn |
| `source-notes-answer-key.md` | Instructor only | The intended themes, the traps, and what a good answer looks like. **Do not share with students before the exercise** |
