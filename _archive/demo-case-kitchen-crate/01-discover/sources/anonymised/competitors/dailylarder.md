# Daily Larder (fictional) — public page

**Price:** from $8.20 per serving. Plans for 2 or 4 people, 3 to 4 dinners a week.

**Delivery:** one fixed weekday for your area, chosen for you. The day cannot be changed.

**Pause and skip:** skip up to 3 weeks per quarter from your account page.

**First box:** includes a printed quick-start card. No in-app onboarding.

**Cooking times:** "Allow about 45 minutes for most dinners."
