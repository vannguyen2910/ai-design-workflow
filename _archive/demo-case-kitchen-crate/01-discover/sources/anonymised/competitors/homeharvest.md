# HomeHarvest (fictional) — public page

**Price:** from $9.50 per serving. Plans for 2 or 4 people, 3 to 5 dinners a week.

**Delivery:** choose any day from Monday to Saturday. You can change the day until 48 hours before delivery. If you are not home, choose "leave in a safe place".

**Pause and skip:** skip any week with one tap on the home screen until 48 hours before delivery. Pause up to 4 weeks.

**First box:** a five-question quiz at sign-up (cooking skill, time, diet). A welcome card in the box.

**Cooking times:** every recipe shows "active time" and "total time".
