How a trip moves through Travel Buddy, step by step

Each column is one actor: the traveler, Travel Buddy itself, a local, and a companion or contact. A line that crosses into another column is a handover. The small line under a step names the planned solution it relies on. Orange marks the steps that no source describes yet.

1. Before the trip: getting started, Discover and Plan

Sign-up is asked for only when the traveler saves a tip or messages someone. How the sign-up screen works and where the crowd signal gets its data are not described anywhere yet.

2. On the trip: arrival and Connect

The chat opens only when both sides have shown interest. There is no search and no invitation yet, so how a traveler finds one specific local is not described anywhere.

3. After the trip: the journal

The journal stays private unless the traveler picks contacts. Removing unwanted records before the journal is built came up in the interview, but no source describes that step yet.
